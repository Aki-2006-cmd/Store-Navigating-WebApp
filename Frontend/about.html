About Aisle_wise

Aisle_wise is a web app that turns a shopping list into the shortest walkable route through the store, with exact aisle, shelf and column directions for every item. It is a university project by Team Apocalypse.

PROJECT TEAM Team Apocalypse
Start Shopping

Our Objective

Wandering through aisles wastes time and crowds the store. Aisle_wise points shoppers straight to each item, so everyone benefits.

Faster Shopping Trips

Guides shoppers along the shortest possible route to checkout, cutting the time spent wandering the aisles.

Precise Product Guidance

Pinpoints each item's exact aisle, shelf and column so shoppers can walk straight to it.

Reduced In-Store Congestion

Smoother, more predictable foot traffic eases crowding in narrow aisles at peak hours.

Higher Customer Satisfaction

A smooth, low-stress shopping experience builds loyalty and repeat visits.

How It Works

From a shopping list to turn-by-turn directions in five steps.

  1. 1

    Shopping List Input

    The shopper enters or uploads their list on the web app.

  2. 2

    Product Location Lookup

    Each item is matched to its aisle, shelf and column.

  3. 3

    Store Graph Model

    The store layout is modeled as a graph of aisles and junctions.

  4. 4

    Shortest-Path Algorithm

    A pathfinding algorithm, such as Dijkstra or A*, puts the stops in the best order.

  5. 5

    Route & Directions

    The shopper gets the optimized path plus the exact location of every item.

Measuring the Impact

These are the targets we will use to check that Aisle_wise delivers on its promises.

20–30%

Reduction in average shopping trip time compared with an unguided trip

≥ 90%

Route optimality: app path length compared with the true shortest path

≥ 95%

Location accuracy: items found at the stated aisle, shelf and column

4.5 / 5

Average user satisfaction score from post-trip surveys

Evaluation methods: timed pilot trials in the store, A/B testing against unguided shopping, and post-trip user satisfaction surveys.
